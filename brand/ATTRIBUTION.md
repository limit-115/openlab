The flask inside the mark is the `flask-conical` glyph from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev),
used unchanged in shape and rescaled to sit with the frame around it.

Lucide is already a dependency of this repository (`lucide-react`), so the mark adds nothing new
to the dependency tree.

## What this means in practice

A mark built on a library glyph cannot be exclusive: anyone may draw the same flask. What is yours
is the frame around it, the proportions, and the name. If the mark ever needs to be defensible on
its own, the flask is the part to redraw — the rest already is.
